Selected work

Three projects that cover the range

Hiding a transformer from its own GPU

The Decepticon attack (IISWC 2023) steals a model's architecture by fingerprinting GPU kernel execution — a trace fine-tuned models inherit from their pre-trained parent. We recreated the attack and built two CUDA-side defenses against it. Constant-load padding cuts the attack's identification rate from 95% to under 10% but costs a 6.6× slowdown; jitter scheduling costs 33% overhead and still cuts it to 61%. Together they bracket a security-performance tradeoff rather than closing it.

Air pollution exposure inequality across U.S. income and racial groups

First-author study introducing two dimensionless diagnostics — the Middle-Group Residual and Within-Stratum Slope Heterogeneity — that recover structure the standard slope index of inequality discards. Built on 24 years of EPA monitor readings for four criteria pollutants, with PM2.5 extended across 44 states by fusing regulatory monitors with population-weighted satellite estimates. The analysis also shows that where the EPA places its sensors biases national estimates enough to reverse the direction of the measured pollution–income trend.

Modular pedalboard with a noise-free distortion pedal

A 63-component, two-layer distortion PCB designed in KiCad around dual TL061 and RC4558 op-amps, with an experimental JFET noise-filter stage to suppress hiss in the analog signal path. Prototyped in LiveSPICE, then hand-assembled — through-hole passives, eight discrete transistors, a seven-diode clipping network, and SOT-23 surface-mount JFETs — and verified by oscilloscope. The surrounding controller handles power and signal routing for any pedal in a standard 1590B enclosure.
